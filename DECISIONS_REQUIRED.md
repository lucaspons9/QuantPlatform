# Research decisions reserved for the researcher

1. Select and license a production-quality historical source and confirm Tiingo account entitlement for the demonstration. No expensive provider is selected or purchased here.
2. Define a point-in-time research universe, security identities and lifecycle policy. The static reference list is biased and illustrative, not an endorsed final universe.
3. Decide handling of complex corporate actions, delistings, missing bars and dividend payment timing. V0 rejects unsupported cases and credits simple dividends ex-date.
4. Predeclare economic hypotheses and future experiment families, including what evidence could justify an alpha claim. The current momentum parameters are conventional workflow defaults, not optimized recommendations.
5. Choose actual trading capacity, calibrated costs, cash yield, tax model and acceptable drawdown/risk before investment interpretation. The bps ladder is illustrative.
6. Decide when to consume a genuine holdout. Default runs stop before test; no holdout results were used for parameter selection. Synthetic test periods are fictional and cannot serve as real validation evidence.
7. Choose reproducible dataset retention and vendor revision policy. Preserve snapshots; do not assume a later download reconstructs the same history.
8. Approve any future allocation of capital. This repository provides no trading connectivity or investment recommendation.
